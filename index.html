Spreading Mental Health's Awareness in Australia

Focusing on the prevalence of mental health illness in Australia in 2021, with an average of ~8-10% across the states. Tasmania (TAS; 9.8%) records the highest percentage of Persons with professionally diagnosed mental health illness, followed closely by South Australia (SA; 9.8%) and Queensland (QLD; 9.6%) respectively. While the Northen Territory (NT) shows the least prevalence, which is almost half of Tasmania, at 5.1%.
